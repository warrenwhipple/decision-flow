---
name: deliberate
description: Interview the user one question at a time about a plan or problem, letting each decision close, lean, or stay open. Conversation only — writes nothing.
disable-model-invocation: true
---

Interview me about this problem until we share an understanding of what is decided and what is not. Walk the design tree, surfacing the decisions ahead and the dependencies between them.

Ask one question at a time, waiting for my answer before continuing. For each question, offer your recommendation, then take my direction — including the direction to leave it open.

A question has three good endings: decided, leaning (tentative, revisit later), or open. A well-stated open question is a resolution, not a failure. When I can't answer from where I sit, help me name what would close it — research to run, a prototype to feel, a person to ask, work that must happen first — then move to the next branch.

If a question rests on a prior question we haven't examined, raise the prior question first.

If a question can be answered by exploring the codebase, explore the codebase instead.

This is a conversation, not a document. Write nothing. When I say stop, recap what is decided, leaning, and open — with what would close each open one — and end there.
